- Source: "Frutiger Aero Wallpapers" by KnightAnNi,
  <https://knightanni.itch.io/frutiger-aero-wallpapers>, archive
  `Frutiger_Aero_4k.zip`.
- Licence: the itch.io page states "This work is marked with CC0 1.0
  Universal" (page captured 2026-10-07). The zip itself holds only the nine
  JPEGs and no licence file, so the page capture is the only record.
- Both files are derivatives, not byte-identical copies: at thumbnail scale
  each differs from its closest original by RMSE ≈ 0.11, so expect a re-grade
  or re-crop. The source match is by image comparison against the zip's
  contents; each matched its listed original more closely than any other.
- `../icons/agents/{shell,agent}.svg` are original drawings made here for
  this song; no vendor logo is reproduced, traced or imitated.
- `../icons/agents/kimi.svg` carries Moonshot AI's mark traced with potrace
  from a reference image the user supplied (8× Lanczos upscale, threshold,
  cropped to the mark). The mark is Moonshot AI's trademark; it
  is used on the user's own desktop by the user's ruling.
- `../icons/agents/hermes.svg` is drawn here after Hermes Agent's CLI emblem
  (Nous Research), from a screenshot the user supplied; it is used on the
  user's own desktop.
- `../icons/agents/claude.svg` and `../icons/agents/codex.svg` are drawn
  here, from memory as plain vector geometry, after Anthropic's Claude spark
  and the OpenAI blossom. Both marks are their owners' trademarks; they are
  used on the user's own desktop by the user's ruling.
- `../icons/agents/eidolon.svg` is drawn here after eidolon's own mark (the
  helmet figure and the wordmark's colours); eidolon is the user's own
  project.
- `../icons/launcher/g3-*.svg` are original work drawn here, inspired by
  the Apple iMac G3 industrial design; no Apple logo. `g3-face.svg` is a
  pixel (・ω・) kaomoji face for the power-on, drawn here.
- `../icons/launcher/finder-*.svg` (folders, the drive, trash, clippings, the
  generic app, the dither and the menu-bar ♪) are original drawings made
  here in the manner of Mac OS 8/9's Platinum icons; no Apple artwork is
  copied.
- `../icons/dock/*.svg` (disclosure triangles, room-log glyphs, the proxy
  and recheck icons) are original drawings made here for the agents dock,
  in the manner of Platinum; no Apple artwork is copied.
- `../icons/tray/*.svg` (the tray's network, volume, flag, agents and
  overflow glyphs, the audio device icons and `apps/fcitx.svg`, a keyboard)
  are original drawings made here in the manner of the Aero tray; no
  Microsoft artwork is copied.
- `../icons/power/*.svg` (switch user, log off, sleep, hibernate, restart,
  shut down) are original drawings made here for the Start menu's power
  flyout, in the manner of the Aero tray glyphs; no Microsoft artwork is
  copied.
- `../icons/user/*.svg` are the Start menu's user pictures, drawn here:
  `chrome-sash.svg`, the default when there is no `~/.face`, is a chrome
  figure taken from the person in `barcarolle-logon.jpg` with a folded
  music staff wrapped behind and over the torso, generated by a script;
  `silhouette.svg` and `pixel-face.svg` are drawn here too.
- `../widgets/Emoji.js` is generated from Unicode's `emoji-test.txt` 17.0
  (Unicode License v3), keeping fully-qualified emoji the installed Noto
  Color Emoji draws; names and subgroups are Unicode's.
- `../widgets/Kaomoji.js` is a list of common kaomoji curated here and
  grouped by mood here; no site's collection is copied. Kaomoji whose first
  strong character is right-to-left are left out, so every entry reads and
  pastes left to right.
- `launcher-finder.png` is a headless Chromium render of the launcher
  screen's design board.
- `launcher-g3.png` is a headless Chromium render of `launcher-g3.html`,
  the G3 launcher's board; its icons and orb are the ones drawn here.
- `agent-icons.png` is the agent icons' contact sheet, a headless Chromium
  render.
- `mock.png` is a headless Chromium render of `mock.html`; every glyph, icon
  and the orb mark in it are drawn here, no Microsoft artwork. Its agent
  icons are the ones above.
